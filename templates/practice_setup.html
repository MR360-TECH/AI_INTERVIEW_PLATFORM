2 Set it up
Viva Voce Settings
The AI examiner will focus 100% of the viva questions on this specific course subject.
Tip: a narrow subject (for example "Operating Systems") gives sharper questions than a broad one.
What a session looks like
ExaminerExplain the difference between a process and a thread.
YouA process has its own memory space, while threads of one process share it...
FluentFlow Settings
The AI will conduct the session based on these preferences in a language-sensitive manner.
Tip: you can answer in the target language or mix in your own; the AI gently corrects as you go.
What a session looks like
ExaminerHello! Tell me about your day, in the language you chose.
YouMy day was busy, but I enjoyed the lunch with my friends...
Concept Drill Settings
The mentor asks open conceptual questions about this topic. Leave it empty to drill general concepts.
Tip: pick one concept (for example "Recursion") rather than a whole subject, so each question can go deeper.
What a session looks like
ExaminerWhat is recursion, and when would you choose it over a loop?
YouRecursion solves a problem by calling itself on a smaller part of it...
Debate Settings
No upfront settings required! The AI will ask you for your chosen debate topic and stance once the session starts.
Example topics: "Social media does more harm than good", "Remote work beats office work", "AI should be allowed in exams".
What a session looks like
ExaminerRemote work beats office work. Argue against it in two lines.
YouOffices build teamwork and make it easier to learn from others...
Healthy Conversation Settings
No upfront settings required! The AI will ask you what you would like to discuss once the session starts.
Good starters: your day, a hobby, a book or film you liked, or a plan you are excited about.
What a session looks like
ExaminerWhat is something you enjoyed this week?
YouI watched a film with my family, and we talked about it for hours...
