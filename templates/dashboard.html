How the Assessment Platform Works
Recruitment Assessment Mode

A formal AI-conducted interview session tailored to your specified domain. The AI examiner evaluates your answers adaptively and generates a certified evaluation report upon completion. Results are recorded in your profile history.

Practice Mode (Ungraded)

A private environment to prepare without evaluation pressure. Includes:

  • Academic Viva Voce — Subject-specific oral exam simulation.
  • FluentFlow — Conversational practice in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Hindi, and more.
  • Debate with AI — Sharp, language-sensitive AI debates in 2 lines per response.
  • Healthy Conversation — Warm, balanced discussions on any topic you choose.
Google & Email OTP Authentication

All candidates authenticate securely via Google Sign-In or Email OTP. Verification codes ensure candidate authenticity for placement reports.
