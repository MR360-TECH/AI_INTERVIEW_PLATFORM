How This Platform Works

Everything you need to know about using this platform.

Recruitment Assessment

This is a formal AI-conducted interview session tailored to your profile and the domain you choose. The AI examiner asks adaptive questions based on your responses, just like a real recruiter would. At the end of your session, a detailed evaluation report is generated and saved to your records. Results from this mode are visible to administrators.

Practice Mode

A private, low-pressure space to sharpen your skills before the real thing. Practice results are never reported to administrators — they are for your personal growth only. You can choose from three practice formats:

  • Academic Viva Voce — Simulates an oral exam environment. Ideal for university students preparing for subject-specific viva sessions.
  • FluentFlow — Improve speaking and communication skills in any language (English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Hindi, and more) with custom practice focus categories like grammar, conversation, or fundamentals.
  • General Concept Drill — A focused Q&A session on any topic you choose. Great for revising concepts, theory, or technical knowledge at your own pace.
How the AI Examiner Works

The AI interviewer adapts its questions based on your previous answers, keeping the conversation natural and relevant. It asks follow-ups when needed, clarifies questions if you are unsure, and adjusts its depth based on the mode you are in. At the end of each session, it evaluates your overall performance and generates a personalised summary report.
