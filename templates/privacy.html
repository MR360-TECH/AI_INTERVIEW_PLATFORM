Section 02
Data We Collect

We collect only the data needed to run the platform:

  • Account and profile: your name, e-mail address, sign-in method, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), and the profile details you enter: gender, education, course, semester, skills, GitHub and LinkedIn links, and (for professionals) years of experience and designation. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name and e-mail address.
  • Resume: the file you optionally upload (PDF or Word, up to 10 MB), and the text and short summary the AI extracts from it.
  • Interview content: the questions asked, your answers (typed, dictated or code), the saved progress of an unfinished interview, and, for scored assessments, your score, evaluation, domain, session code and time. Files you attach to an answer are analysed by the AI; the file itself is not kept, only a short text analysis in the answer record.
  • Integrity data (scored assessments only): a record of each rule violation (type, time, question number, strike number, and details such as how many seconds you were away), your strike count, a random identifier that tells browsers apart, when each question was shown and answered, and typing-activity counts (how many key presses, how long an answer took, whether voice was used). We do not record what you type key by key, and the platform does not use your camera or record your screen or audio.
  • Security data: your IP address and failed sign-in counters (to limit guessing attacks), and one-time codes, which are stored only as short-lived hashes.
  • Communications: e-mails we send you, and any feedback you submit (rating, topic, message and whether you allow a reply).
  • Library activity: links you bookmark. Study-plan ticks stay in your own browser.

We do not collect payment information, government identifiers or other sensitive personal data. Please do not put highly sensitive information in your answers or resume.

Section 05
Storage, Security & Retention

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Neon) and the application runs on Render. Passwords are salted and hashed with Werkzeug and are never stored in plain text. One-time codes are stored only as keyed hashes and expire after 10 minutes.

Security measures include:

  • HTTPS in production with HSTS, a Content-Security-Policy and other security headers.
  • Session cookies that are HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure; an idle timeout (administrators 30 minutes, candidates 2 hours) and a 12-hour maximum.
  • A secret token on every form and request (CSRF protection), limits on repeated sign-in and code attempts, and password strength rules.
  • Upload checks for type and size; access to signed-in pages requires a valid server-side session.

Retention: account data, results and integrity records are kept for as long as your account exists. An unfinished interview is deleted when it ends or when you exit it; if you exit, the questions and answers you gave are kept with that session's record for the administrator. Resume files are kept until you remove them or your account is deleted. Security counters and codes expire automatically.

No internet system is completely free of risk. If we become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will take reasonable steps to limit harm and to inform affected users as required by law.

Section 07
Examination Integrity & Automated Proctoring

To keep scored assessments fair, the platform applies integrity rules during a live scored assessment. Practice labs are never proctored. The administrator can switch each rule on or off and sets the strike limit (1 to 5, default 2). The rules are:

  • Stay in the exam window: switching to another tab, window or app is a violation.
  • No pasting or copying: pasting into the answer box or copying the question text is blocked and is a violation.
  • One session: opening the same interview in another browser or device while it is active is refused and is a violation.
  • Time limit: the clock is kept by the server, and answers that arrive well after the limit are a violation.
  • Fullscreen (only when the administrator requires it): leaving fullscreen is a violation.
  • Typing-pattern review: long answers that arrive instantly or appear to be inserted rather than typed may be flagged for the administrator. A flag is not a strike and nothing happens automatically.

Every violation is one strike. You always see a box that names the exact mistake, your strike count and a warning. When you reach the strike limit, the session ends automatically and is recorded as Terminated with a score of 0/10; a terminated session counts as one attempt.

Audit log: violations and review flags are stored with the assessment record and are visible to the administrator only. They are not shown on the candidate's own report. If you believe a violation was recorded in error, contact us (see Contact) and quote your session code.
